Privacy Policy

Social Pipeline · Last updated November 3, 2026

Social Pipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner, Quinn Lawery. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Social Pipeline reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheets, Google Docs, Google Calendar events and Google Drive files that make up the content calendar and video scripts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to plan, schedule and record the owner's social media posts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or transferred to anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Social Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
